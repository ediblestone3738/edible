## Overview: Navigating the Budapest Metro

Budapest boasts one of the most efficient and historic underground rail networks in Europe. Whether you are arriving at Keleti railway station or traveling between Buda and Pest, taking the metro is by far the fastest and most affordable way to get around the city.

The network consists of **four main lines**:
* **M1 (Yellow Line):** The historic Millennium Underground Railway (continental Europe's oldest subway line), connecting Vörösmarty tér to City Park.
* **M2 (Red Line):** Connects Buda (Déli pályaudvar) with Pest (Keleti pályaudvar), stopping at the Hungarian Parliament (Kossuth Lajos tér).
* **M3 (Blue Line):** Crucial line spanning north to south on the Pest side, connecting major hub stations like Deák Ferenc tér.
* **M4 (Green Line):** Modern automated line connecting Kelenföld railway station to Keleti station, stopping at the Grand Market Hall (Fővám tér).

Welcome to our complete guide on how to use the Budapest metro system. Navigating public transportation in a new country can feel intimidating, but Budapest makes it straightforward once you understand how the tickets and validation work.

### Buying Tickets and Passes

When using the Budapest transport system (BKK), you have several options:
1. **Single Tickets:** Valid for a single unbroken journey on the metro lines.
2. **Time-based Tickets (30-minute or 90-minute):** Allow unlimited transfers within the designated time frame, provided you validate the ticket at the start of your journey.
3. **Daily Travelcards (24-hour or 72-hour):** Best value if you plan to make more than 4 or 5 journeys per day.

You can purchase physical paper tickets from purple BKK vending machines at all major metro stations (credit cards and contactless payments are accepted), or digitally using the official **BudapestGO** app.

### Ticket Validation & Ticket Inspectors

> **Important Rule:** Having a ticket is not enough—you must **validate** it before entering the platform area.

* **Paper Tickets:** Insert your paper single ticket into the orange or yellow validating machines at the entrance to the metro station before descending the escalators. Keep your stamped ticket until you exit your destination station.
* **Digital Tickets:** Scan the QR code displayed on the validating machines or on the sides of the metro entrance gates using the BudapestGO app before entering the platform.

Plainclothes and uniformed ticket inspectors frequently check tickets at station exits and on platforms. Unvalidated tickets carry an immediate on-the-spot fine, so double-check that your ticket has been stamped or scanned!

### Transferring Between Lines

All major lines intersect at **Deák Ferenc tér** (M1, M2, and M3 meet here), making line transfers seamless. If you are using a standard single paper ticket, note that switching lines inside Deák Ferenc tér is allowed on a single ticket, provided you do not exit the station turnstiles or gates.

## Summary Tips for Travelers

1. Download the **BudapestGO** app prior to your trip for live transit maps and mobile ticket purchases.
2. Always validate single paper tickets in the stamping boxes at the top of the escalators.
3. Keep your ticket handy until you have fully exited the station building.
